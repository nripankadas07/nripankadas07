## vcd-state-ledger

User: RTL verification engineers reviewing a short simulation capture.

Pain: A plot alone does not provide a reproducible unknown-state duration ledger across aliased signals.

Acceptance: Exact duration conservation, aliases, same-tick glitches, scalar edges and x/z propagation; reject undeclared identifiers, invalid widths, incomplete scopes and decreasing timestamps.

Invented example: The example intentionally exits 1: bus unknown bit-time is 10 bit-ticks. Clock 0/1 durations are 10/5 ticks; bus 0/1/x durations are 10/10/10 bit-ticks. Aliases share one signal record.

Validation: 20 behavior methods; compilation, wheel build and a clean installed-command check outside the checkout, including meaningful failure cases. Final Linux/Python 3.10, 3.12 and 3.14 CI passed. [PR #1](https://github.com/nripankadas07/vcd-state-ledger/pull/1), [research/tradeoffs](https://github.com/nripankadas07/vcd-state-ledger/blob/17ee488c96d35063e6f042c2d61ec6ebfbd8b4e4/RESEARCH.md), [validation](https://github.com/nripankadas07/vcd-state-ledger/blob/17ee488c96d35063e6f042c2d61ec6ebfbd8b4e4/VALIDATION.md), [preparation](https://github.com/nripankadas07/vcd-state-ledger/blob/17ee488c96d35063e6f042c2d61ec6ebfbd8b4e4/PREPARATION.json).

Limits: 2 MiB, 250,000 tokens, 512 unique IDs, 2,000 names, 100,000 value events and scope depth 32. No real values, EVCD, FST, dump-on/off intervals, analog timing or power inference. This is a deliberately small capture parser, not a waveform viewer.

## obj-island-audit

User: CAD/export pipeline maintainers inspecting a triangulated OBJ before downstream processing.

Pain: Disconnected parts, seam duplicates and orientation conflicts need attributable face/edge evidence rather than an unexplained pass/fail.

Acceptance: Recover two closed islands, attribute boundary/nonmanifold/orientation/contact defects, validate interleaved relative indices and reject forward/zero references and unsupported polygons.

Invented example: The invented OBJ has two separate closed tetrahedral edge surfaces, eight faces and no reported edge defects; exit 0. Multiple closed islands alone are not an error.

Validation: 21 behavior methods; compilation, wheel build and a clean installed-command check outside the checkout, including meaningful failure cases. Final Linux/Python 3.10, 3.12 and 3.14 CI passed. [PR #1](https://github.com/nripankadas07/obj-island-audit/pull/1), [research/tradeoffs](https://github.com/nripankadas07/obj-island-audit/blob/6932a0807879693cad77e5e4f31d177bbdd631d4/RESEARCH.md), [validation](https://github.com/nripankadas07/obj-island-audit/blob/6932a0807879693cad77e5e4f31d177bbdd631d4/VALIDATION.md), [preparation](https://github.com/nripankadas07/obj-island-audit/blob/6932a0807879693cad77e5e4f31d177bbdd631d4/PREPARATION.json).

Limits: 2 MiB, 20,000 vertices and 30,000 triangle faces. Coordinates: zero or absolute magnitude 1e-12 through 1e9, bounded decimal precision. No polygons, freeform curves, repair, self-intersection detection, vertex-manifold certification, solid volume or printability guarantee. closed_edge_surface only means each retained component edge has incidence two; it is not a full watertightness claim. Area is approximate.

## modbus-frame-ledger

User: Controls engineers reviewing an offline serial capture with a device register map.

Pain: A CRC-valid byte sequence still needs explicit address context, byte order, scale and coverage evidence.

Acceptance: Verify known CRC vectors, all byte orders, signed values, binary32 scaling, complete map coverage, CRC failure and valid exception/nonfinite/out-of-range findings.

Invented example: Exit 0, with temperature -10, status 0 and pressure 12.5. All four captured registers are covered.

Validation: 18 behavior methods; compilation, wheel build and a clean installed-command check outside the checkout, including meaningful failure cases. Final Linux/Python 3.10, 3.12 and 3.14 CI passed. [PR #1](https://github.com/nripankadas07/modbus-frame-ledger/pull/1), [research/tradeoffs](https://github.com/nripankadas07/modbus-frame-ledger/blob/19cf250a7c17cbfa84ef440b7e6842de39be209a/RESEARCH.md), [validation](https://github.com/nripankadas07/modbus-frame-ledger/blob/19cf250a7c17cbfa84ef440b7e6842de39be209a/VALIDATION.md), [preparation](https://github.com/nripankadas07/modbus-frame-ledger/blob/19cf250a7c17cbfa84ef440b7e6842de39be209a/PREPARATION.json).

Limits: One frame of 5–256 bytes and at most 125 registers; CSV 64 KiB and 125 rows. No overlapping mapped spans, serial ports, TCP, polling, device writes, request capture pairing, authentication or device-specific register semantics. Exceptions, unmapped words, map rows outside the response and nonfinite floats produce valid exit-1 evidence. Exact numeric representation is not a claim about sensor measurement precision.

## cue-sample-plan

User: Audio archivists checking how a CUE sheet would divide an uncompressed disc image.

Pain: A split plan should explain recorded gaps and account for every source frame before any encoder runs.

Acceptance: Prove exact contiguous frame coverage, explicit INDEX00 gaps and leading findings, stereo byte offsets, source-byte preservation, RIFF validation, and rejection of unrepresentable frame boundaries and unsafe paths.

Invented example: The generator writes invented silence into a new directory. The plan exits 0 and covers [0,44100) track 1, [44100,88200) recorded pregap, and [88200,176400) track 2. Planning does not modify source files.

Validation: 20 behavior methods; compilation, wheel build and a clean installed-command check outside the checkout, including meaningful failure cases. Final Linux/Python 3.10, 3.12 and 3.14 CI passed. [PR #1](https://github.com/nripankadas07/cue-sample-plan/pull/1), [research/tradeoffs](https://github.com/nripankadas07/cue-sample-plan/blob/094762d93f180656f4aac9d7b50e99938a4b058f/RESEARCH.md), [validation](https://github.com/nripankadas07/cue-sample-plan/blob/094762d93f180656f4aac9d7b50e99938a4b058f/VALIDATION.md), [preparation](https://github.com/nripankadas07/cue-sample-plan/blob/094762d93f180656f4aac9d7b50e99938a4b058f/PREPARATION.json).

Limits: CUE 64 KiB, 16 local files, 99 tracks, 64 MiB per WAVE/128 MiB aggregate. No symlinks or path traversal, compressed audio, RF64/RIFX/extensible WAVE, synthetic PREGAP/POSTGAP, extra indices, copying, transcoding, resampling, tags or audio quality assessment. Unknown RIFF chunks are counted. --make-example only creates a new directory and refuses overwrite.

## pgn-clock-ledger

User: Chess study authors auditing recorded clock annotations in a short standard-start game.

Pain: Missing annotations or an unexplained clock increase should not silently become zero thinking time.

Acceptance: Reproduce increment arithmetic, fractional milliseconds, per-side missing-clock resynchronization, ignored variations, and reject duplicate/leading clocks, malformed controls, wrong move numbers and result conflicts.

Invented example: Exit 0 with elapsed milliseconds [3000,2000,3000,5000]; known totals are white 6000 and black 7000 under the stated increment convention.

Validation: 20 behavior methods; compilation, wheel build and a clean installed-command check outside the checkout, including meaningful failure cases. Final Linux/Python 3.10, 3.12 and 3.14 CI passed. [PR #1](https://github.com/nripankadas07/pgn-clock-ledger/pull/1), [research/tradeoffs](https://github.com/nripankadas07/pgn-clock-ledger/blob/76fffed24a0495e241770ab9cee8110b699c416e/RESEARCH.md), [validation](https://github.com/nripankadas07/pgn-clock-ledger/blob/76fffed24a0495e241770ab9cee8110b699c416e/VALIDATION.md), [preparation](https://github.com/nripankadas07/pgn-clock-ledger/blob/76fffed24a0495e241770ab9cee8110b699c416e/PREPARATION.json).

Limits: 128 KiB, 64 headers, 10,000 plies, variation depth 32, token length 64; base 1–86400 seconds and increment 0–3600. No multi-stage/delay controls, FEN starts, variants, multiple games or sub-millisecond clocks. Move labels are not checked for chess legality. No engine, cheating, flag, network lag or actual-thinking-time inference; totals cover only computed evidence.
