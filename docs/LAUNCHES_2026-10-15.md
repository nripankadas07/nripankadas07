### backup-chain-audit

**User:** Backup operators reviewing an explicit base/incremental manifest and a retention/deletion proposal.

**Pain:** Deleting one base can strand multiple retained incrementals; total storage can double-count shared ancestors.

**Acceptance:** A shared ancestor is counted once; a proposed deletion of base is blocked; missing, unverified and cyclic ancestry withhold deletion eligibility; duplicate identities are rejected.

**Limits:** Not an adapter for restic, Borg, Duplicati or any backup format. No backup bytes, checksums, encryption, availability, retention calendar or actual restore is verified. Caller-supplied verified=true is evidence to review, not proof. No files are deleted. Empty retain means no preservation roots were requested. Corruption outside selected chains is not exhaustively assessed.

### kafka-offset-audit

**User:** Streaming operators reviewing exported topic-partition bounds and consumer commits before an offset reset.

**Pain:** A negative-looking lag can hide a commit ahead of the captured end; retention can invalidate a previously committed next offset.

**Acceptance:** Start/end boundaries are valid; retention gaps and ahead-of-end commits are distinguished; missing commits with reset=none stay unknown; requested moves identify skip/replay; duplicate partitions and booleans as offsets fail.

**Limits:** Captured JSON only; no broker connection, consumer reset, group-membership check, leader-epoch validation or transaction/isolation behavior. log_end is the caller-supplied end boundary. Offset distance is not a record count: compaction, gaps, control records and transactions matter. Nonatomic captures may be inconsistent. No data-loss or replay guarantee is implied.

### invoice-rounding-ledger

**User:** Billing engineers reconciling line rounding against tax-group rounding, including credit lines.

**Pain:** Individually rounded tax lines can disagree with a rounded group total; a correction should conserve the total and have deterministic tie-breaking.

**Acceptance:** Allocated tax sums to each rounded group for positive, negative and mixed-sign inputs; permutation does not change id-based allocation; equivalent decimal rates coalesce; discount/quantity math is exact; duplicate ids and float/exponent values fail.

**Limits:** Arithmetic contract only. No jurisdiction-specific tax rules, currency exponent lookup, FX, inclusive taxes, compound taxes, shipping or invoice importers. Caller supplies rates and digits. Allocations can differ by a minor unit from individually rounded tax lines; this is explicit, not a legal/accounting correctness claim. No invoices or payments are sent.

### otel-span-witness

**User:** Observability engineers inspecting a small captured trace after sampling or instrumentation changes.

**Pain:** Missing parents, cyclic ancestry and overlapping child intervals can make a trace view misleading; timestamp arithmetic should not be confused with measured self latency.

**Acceptance:** Overlaps do not double count; clipping stays within parent duration; cycles are emitted once per component; same span id in different traces is allowed; missing parents are explicit; async lifetimes are annotated without being rejected.

**Limits:** Simplified explicit JSON contract, not an OTLP/protobuf/Jaeger importer. No collector, remote lookup, span-link/event analysis, clock synchronization or semantic-convention validation. Absent sampled/remote parents may be legitimate. Child coverage is not CPU time or measured self latency; cross-service clocks may differ. No production trace is sent anywhere.

### matrix-include-trace

**User:** CI maintainers reviewing how literal matrix includes and excludes change job coverage.

**Pain:** An include can overwrite an earlier added value while being forbidden to overwrite original axes; appended jobs are not merged by later includes.

**Acceptance:** Earlier added values may be overwritten without changing original axes; appended rows stay independent; excluded combinations can be re-added as appended entries; include-only and256-job boundary work; expressions and product overflow fail.

**Limits:** Literal JSON subset only, not YAML or a complete GitHub Actions evaluator. No expressions, object axes, reusable workflows, job if/needs, runner resolution, fail-fast, concurrency, secret handling or execution. Exact scalar typing and rejected duplicate axes are declared limits; do not treat this as a platform conformance certificate.
