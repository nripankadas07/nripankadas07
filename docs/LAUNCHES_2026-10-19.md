## Limits

FITS supports primary/IMAGE extents without checksum or pixel interpretation. ELF page unions are a declared model, not OS loader simulation. TIFF supports a small uncompressed grayscale subset. Cutting-stock uses a specified between-piece kerf model, not machine instructions. RUP is a bounded educational checker, not full DRAT or formal assurance. Production datasets, Windows/macOS and competitor performance were not tested.
